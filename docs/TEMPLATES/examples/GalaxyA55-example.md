<badge>Official diagram</badge> **Location:** upper rear area, to the right of the camera assembly.

**Evidence:** The Galaxy A55 5G user guide's device-layout diagram explicitly labels the NFC antenna. The manual's NFC instructions also say to place the NFC antenna area on the back of the device near the NFC tag.

> **Important distinction:** this is the location of the NFC antenna, not necessarily the NFC controller/chip. The controller's exact component/package location is not identified by this user-guide diagram.

- **Evidence type:** manufacturer user-guide device-layout illustration.
